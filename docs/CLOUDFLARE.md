## Walkthrough for Mich

1. Sign in to [Cloudflare](https://dash.cloudflare.com/), select the account containing the domain, then open **dragons.market**.
2. Open **DNS > Records** in the sidebar, then click **Add record**.
3. Choose **CNAME**. For the first row below, enter **Name** `@` and **Target** `yz9kq6zy.up.railway.app`. The `@` means the main domain, `dragons.market`.
4. Click the orange cloud so **Proxy status** reads **DNS only** with a grey cloud. Leave **TTL** on **Auto**, then click **Save**.
5. Repeat **Add record** for the other two CNAME rows, using `api` and `mcp` as their names. Each row is a separate record; keep all three grey-clouded.
6. Add the three TXT rows individually. Choose **TXT**, copy the row's **Name** and full **Content**, leave **TTL** on **Auto**, then **Save**. TXT records have no proxy switch; that is normal.
7. Check all six rows against the table, then tell Filmer they are saved so he can verify the domains and HTTPS certificates in Railway.

Use the short names exactly as shown; do not append `.dragons.market` yourself. These steps follow Cloudflare's [DNS record instructions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/dns/manage-dns-records/how-to/create-dns-records/).

Paste the full TXT contents, without adding quotation marks. CNAME targets contain no `https://`, port or path. Replace a conflicting A/AAAA/CNAME only at these exact hostnames; leave mail and other records alone.

Cloudflare supports the root `@` CNAME through [CNAME flattening](https://developers.cloudflare.com/dns/cname-flattening/). Keep these records DNS only for this setup; Railway terminates HTTPS and issues the certificates.
