Information we use

The information involved depends on how you use the site:

  • Wallet information. Connecting a supported wallet makes your public account identifier available to the site. Public balances, positions and transaction records may be retrieved to display your account activity. Your wallet manages its own credentials.
  • Market and Council records. The site reads market questions, prices, results and public dispute records from the configured network. Those records can include account identifiers, evidence, votes and reasons for a decision.
  • Browser preferences. Your theme choice and saved market identifiers are stored on your device. Watchlists are associated with the connected account in that browser; they are not currently synchronised through a Dragons Market account database.
  • Policy acceptance. When you agree to the Terms and acknowledge this policy, the site stores the public wallet account, network, document versions and a browser-generated timestamp locally. This is a browser record of your choice, without a wallet signature or a central acceptance database.
  • Connection information. Serving a page or answering a network request exposes information such as an IP address, browser headers, request time and requested resource to the relevant hosting or network provider. Those providers may keep operational and security logs.
  • Information you send. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include. Keep messages relevant to your request and avoid sending unnecessary personal or financial information.

The current site does not ask you to create a password-based profile or submit identity documents, card details or precise device location. If a future feature needs additional information, its requirements and privacy notice will be provided before collection.

Why information is used

Information is used to show markets and account activity, remember your choices, retrieve public records, investigate technical problems, protect the service and respond to requests. Information may also be used when necessary to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

Access to support or dispute information must be limited to the purpose for which it is needed. Providing information for a case does not authorise unrelated advertising, public profiling or AI-model training. If a new use requires a separate legal basis or consent, it must be addressed before that use begins.

Where a law requires a legal basis, the basis depends on the purpose: providing a feature you request may be necessary to perform a contract; preventing abuse and maintaining the service may rely on legitimate interests after considering your rights; legally required processing relies on the relevant legal obligation. Optional processing that requires consent must be offered separately. Reading this policy or connecting a wallet is not blanket consent to unrelated processing.

Cookies and browser storage

The current frontend uses local storage to remember your theme, the markets you save and your policy acceptance. This storage stays in that browser until you remove it or clear the site’s data. Disconnecting a wallet does not automatically erase those records or revoke permissions granted inside your wallet. Clearing an acceptance record means you will be asked to review the policies again.

The current frontend has no advertising pixels or third-party audience analytics integration. Wallet software and linked services can use their own storage under their own policies. We will explain any additional tracking and request consent before enabling it where required by law.

You can clear site data through your browser settings. Doing so resets preferences and locally saved watchlists, but does not change blockchain records or the assets in your wallet.

Who can receive information

Hosting providers receive the requests needed to deliver the site. Blockchain RPC services receive queries used to retrieve markets, balances, transactions or dispute records. A query can contain the public account or contract being looked up; a directly contacted provider can also see your connection information.

If you connect a wallet, the wallet and connection service process the information needed for that connection under their own terms. If transaction features are enabled, the selected network and any provider involved in that transaction receive the information needed to process it.

Information may be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to address fraud, threats or legal claims, subject to applicable law. It may also be transferred as part of a service transfer or restructuring, with applicable privacy obligations continuing to apply.

Dragons Market does not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. An external link, including a partner link, takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices. We do not send a saved watchlist to a partner merely because that partner is listed on this site.

Public blockchain records

A public account identifier is not the same as anonymity. Transfers, contract interactions, market positions and published Council activity can be linked together and may identify you when combined with other information.

Data recorded on a public blockchain can be copied, indexed and retained by independent participants around the world. We cannot erase or change those independent records. Removing something from this website does not remove it from a blockchain, explorer or another person’s copy.

Only submit evidence or other public content that you have the right to disclose. Avoid including identity documents, private correspondence or personal details that are unnecessary to resolve a market.

Content about political, religious or health-related events may permit sensitive inferences about an identifiable person. A public record is not proof of someone’s beliefs. Keep unnecessary personal details out of market questions, supporting evidence and juror explanations, especially information about children or people who are not participating.

The technical limits of a blockchain do not remove obligations over information under our control. Where required, we must consider measures such as reducing information collected, limiting website display or correcting information we publish, even when an independent chain record cannot be changed.

Retention and security

Retention of information under our control is limited by its purpose, including security, resolving requests and meeting legal obligations. Relevant considerations include the sensitivity of the information, the duration of a request or dispute and any legally required retention period. Information no longer needed must be deleted or anonymised, subject to lawful preservation requirements.

A specific legal claim, investigation or preservation duty may require relevant records to be kept longer. That does not justify retaining every record indefinitely or using preserved information for an unrelated purpose. The locally stored policy acknowledgement is not a centrally retained or independently verified acceptance record.

Local preferences follow the browser-storage controls described above. Public blockchain records and independent provider records follow the rules and technical limitations of those systems.

No online service can guarantee complete security. Protect your device and wallet, review connection permissions and never disclose a recovery phrase or private key. The website requests wallet interactions through supported wallet software; it does not need you to enter those secrets into a website form.

If personal information under our control is involved in a security incident, applicable investigation, containment and notification duties still apply. A blockchain loss or wallet incident may also require action with the relevant provider; this policy does not promise that every independent provider incident can be reversed.

International processing

Hosting, wallet and blockchain services may process information outside your country. Privacy protections and legal access rules differ between countries. Public blockchain activity is distributed globally rather than stored in a single location controlled by this website.

Transfers of personal information under our control remain subject to applicable data-protection requirements. Where a transfer requires an adequacy decision, approved contractual safeguards or another lawful mechanism, that requirement is not replaced by your use of the site. This policy does not claim that one transfer mechanism covers every provider or country.

Your rights and choices

Depending on your location and the law that applies, you may have rights to obtain information about processing, access or receive a copy of your data, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision about a request. You may also complain to the data-protection authority or other regulator available to you.

These rights can be subject to verification, legal exceptions and the response periods set by local law. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. A deletion request does not give this website the ability to alter an independent public blockchain, but that limitation does not remove rights over information we control.

You can disconnect your wallet, manage permissions in the wallet and remove local site data in your browser. You do not have to connect a wallet to read public market information. Exercising an applicable privacy right should not result in unlawful discrimination.

Privacy-request contact details will be published here before the public launch. No recovery phrase or private key is needed for a privacy request.

Age limits and policy updates

Dragons Market is intended for adults who meet the age and eligibility requirements in the Terms of Use. It is not directed to children. Personal information received from a child must be handled in accordance with applicable deletion and protection requirements.

We may update this policy as features, providers or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest version. Material changes will be brought to users’ attention before they take effect where required, and new consent will be requested where necessary.
